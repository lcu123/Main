Privacy Policy

Zest Video is the app {{ brand }} technicians use to record a short video after a service visit. This policy covers the app and the web page our customers open to watch their video.

What the app collects

Video and sound. One short clip per technician per visit, recorded by the technician on purpose. It shows the property and the technician's own voice. The screen shows a red border and a timer the whole time it records. Nothing is recorded at any other time.

Location. The phone's location when the technician opens his list of stops and when a recording starts, to suggest the right stop and to check the video is for the right address. If reminders are turned on, the phone also notices when he leaves a stop, so it can remind him to record. Location is not tracked at any other time and is not used for ads.

Speech. The phone turns the clip's speech into text, on the phone itself, to check that the customer's name or street was said. That text is kept with the video so our office can match a video to the right customer.

Sign-in details. The technician's work mobile number or email, a one-time code we send to it by text message or email, and a token that keeps that phone signed in.

Device details. The phone model, iOS version, app version and a notification token, so we can fix problems and send a reminder when a video is waiting to upload.

What the watch page collects

When you open your video link we note that the video was played. If you leave a score or a comment, we keep it and share it with our office. The page sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

The video is sent to the customer whose property was serviced, by a private link. We use location and speech only to make sure a video reaches the right customer. We do not sell this information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track anyone across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that run the service for us: our hosting and storage provider, our email and text message providers, Apple (for notifications), and FieldRoutes, the system we use to manage service visits. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Videos are stored privately and deleted after {{ retention_months }} months, or sooner if our office removes them. A video leaves the technician's phone as soon as it is uploaded and never goes into the phone's photo library.

Customers

You do not need an account to watch your video. The link is private to you; please do not share it if you do not want others to see the video. Scores and comments go to our office and are not posted publicly. Every video email has a link to stop these emails.

Your choices and deleting your data

Customers: to have a video removed, to stop video emails, or to ask what we hold about you, {{ reach_us() }}.

Technicians: you can sign out in the app at any time, which removes this phone's access. Sign-in is provided by {{ brand }} as part of your work; to have your sign-in details and device details deleted, {{ reach_us() }}. Access is removed automatically when you no longer work with us.

You can turn off the camera, microphone, location, speech recognition and notifications for the app at any time in the iPhone's Settings. The app needs the camera and microphone to record.

Children

The app is for adult employees. It is not directed to children, and technicians are asked to keep children and other people out of the picture.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new version here. Questions: {{ reach_us() }}.

Support

Zest Video is for {{ brand }} technicians. Sign in with the mobile number on your work record. We text you a 6-digit code.

Technicians

No code arrived? Check you typed the mobile number the office has for you, then ask for a new code. If it still does not come, call the office: your number may need to be added or fixed.

A video says Waiting? It sends by itself when you have signal. Keep the app installed and open it once you are back in range.

Recorded the wrong house? For 10 minutes you can delete it or record again in the app. After that, call the office.

Want to stop using the app? Open the My Videos tab and tap Sign out. To have your details deleted, call the office.
