Privacy

Zest Video is the app our technicians use to record a short video after a service visit. This page covers the app and the video page our customers open.

What the app collects

Video and sound. One short clip per visit, recorded by the technician on purpose. It shows the property and the technician's own voice.

Location. The phone's location when a recording starts, to check the video is for the right address. If the technician turns on reminders, the phone also notices when he leaves a stop so it can remind him to record. Location is not tracked at any other time.

Speech. The phone turns the clip's speech into text, on the phone, to check the customer's name or street was said.

Sign-in details. The technician's work phone number or email, and a token that keeps that phone signed in.

How it is used

The video is sent to the customer whose property was serviced, by a private link. We use the location and speech only to make sure a video reaches the right customer. We do not sell this information, we do not use it for ads, and we do not track anyone across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Videos are stored privately and deleted after {{ retention_months }} months, or sooner if our office removes them. Videos leave the technician's phone as soon as they are uploaded and never go into the phone's photo library.

Customers

You do not need an account to watch your video. The link is private to you. If you leave a score or a comment, it goes to our office and is not posted publicly. Every video email has a link to stop these emails.

Your choices

To have a video removed, to stop video emails, or to ask what we hold about you, contact us{% if privacy_contact %} at {{ privacy_contact }}{% endif %}{% if office_phone %}{% if privacy_contact %} or{% endif %} call {{ office_phone | phone }}{% endif %}. Technicians can sign out in the app at any time; our office removes access when someone leaves.

Support

Zest Video is for {{ brand }} technicians. Sign in with the mobile number or email on your work record and the 6-digit code we send you.

Technicians

No code arrived? Check the number or email is the one on your work record, then ask for a new code.

A video says Waiting? It sends by itself when you have signal. Keep the app installed and open it once you are back in range.

Recorded the wrong house? For 10 minutes you can delete it or record again in the app. After that, call the office.

Customers

If your video will not play, check your connection and tap play again. If the video shows a property that is not yours, tap "This is not my home" under the video.
