Techs and phones

Techs come from FieldRoutes: every active employee whose type is Technician. A tech signs in by himself: he types the mobile number on his FieldRoutes employee record and gets a code by text. A tech with no mobile number there cannot get a text; add it in FieldRoutes, press Refresh, and he can sign in. Mark him inactive in FieldRoutes and his app locks within a day; cut off a phone here and it locks at once.
