# Zest Video: instructions for techs

You record one short video at every stop. Setting up takes about 10 minutes,
once. After that each video takes about 30 seconds.

Checked against the app's own screens and Apple's TestFlight pages on
Nov 16, 2026. Apple's own button words (Allow, OK) differ a little between
iPhone versions.

## Before you start

You need three things.

- **An iPhone on iOS 17 or newer.** Check in Settings, General, Software
  Update. If the number is under 17, update the phone. If no update is
  offered, the phone is too old: tell the office.
- **Your Apple account email.** Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  The email under your name is the one to give the office.
- **Your mobile number on file.** The office must have the same mobile number
  you will type into the app.

The app is for iPhone only.

## Get the app

The app is not in the App Store yet. Apple sends you two emails, and you
install it through Apple's free TestFlight app. Do every step on your iPhone,
not on a computer.

1. **First email, from Apple.** It says you were invited to App Store Connect.
   Tap the button in it. Sign in with the Apple account you use for the App
   Store on this phone. Type the 6-digit code if Apple asks for one. Do this
   within 3 days.
2. **Tell the office: "I accepted."** The second email cannot come before the
   office adds you.
3. **Install TestFlight.** Open the App Store, search for **TestFlight**, tap
   **Get**. Open it once and tap **Continue**. Allow notifications.
4. **Second email, from TestFlight, about Zest Video.** Open it and tap
   **View in TestFlight**.
5. TestFlight opens. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**. Wait, then tap **Open**.

The app is now on your Home Screen as **Zest Video**. A small orange dot sits
in front of its name. That only means it came through TestFlight.

Keep the TestFlight app. It brings you new versions. If it asks about
automatic updates, say yes.

## Sign in

You sign in once. After that the app remembers you.

1. Open **Zest Video**. The first time, TestFlight may show a short note of
   its own. Tap **Next**, then **Start Testing**.
2. Type your mobile number, the one the office has for you. Tap
   **SEND MY CODE**.
3. A text arrives with 6 numbers. Type them in. There is no button to tap
   after: the app goes on by itself.

The code works once and lasts 15 minutes. You get 5 tries.

**No text after a minute or two?** Tap **BACK** and check the number. If the
number is wrong the app does not say so; the text just never comes. You can
also tap **SEND A NEW CODE**, or tap **USE EMAIL INSTEAD**. Still nothing:
call the office. They can read you a code.

## The phone asks five questions

Say yes to all five. Each one is asked once.

| When | The phone asks about | Tap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right after your code | Location | **Allow While Using App** |
| The first time you tap a stop | Camera | **Allow** |
| Right after that | Microphone | **Allow** |
| The first time you save a video | Speech Recognition | **Allow** |
| After your first saved video | Notifications | **Allow** |

On some phones the button says **OK** instead of **Allow**.

The app cannot record without the camera and the microphone. Location and
speech let the app check the video goes to the right home. If you say no to
those, the app asks "Is this the right stop?" far more often.

**Tapped the wrong answer?** Open the iPhone's Settings, find **Zest Video**,
and switch it on there.

## At every stop

Six steps, about 30 seconds.

1. **Open the app and tap your stop.** Your closest stop is at the top. The
   whole card is the button.
2. **Check the name and address.** Right: tap **THIS IS IT**. Wrong: tap
   **WRONG STOP**.
3. **Point the phone at the property and tap the big red button.**
4. **Talk for 15 seconds.** The screen shows what to say, one line at a time:
    - **Hi** and the customer's first name. Say the name out loud. The app
      listens for it.
    - Here's what I **did**
    - Here's what I **recommend**
    - I **closed the backyard gate**
5. **It stops by itself.** The video plays back. If you recommended
   something, tap **I recommended something** so it says **YES**.
6. **Tap SAVE.** A green tick and **Saved** show. You are done. Drive on.

The video sends by itself, even with the app closed.

A red border means it is recording. Do not press the volume buttons while you
record: that ends the video early.

Not happy with it? On the playback screen tap **RECORD AGAIN** instead of SAVE.

## Good to know

- **Made a mistake?** You have 10 minutes. Tap the stop (it says
  **CHANGE VIDEO**), then tap **RECORD AGAIN** or **DELETE VIDEO**. The 10
  minutes start when you began recording. After that, call the office.
- **No signal? No problem.** Record and save as usual. The video waits on your
  phone and sends later. Open the app once when you have signal again.
- **Never delete the app while a video is waiting.** The video would be lost.
- **Stop not on your list?** Scroll to the bottom of the list. Tap
  **STOP NOT ON MY LIST**, then **RECORD**. Say the customer's name and the
  address in the video. The office will match it.
- **Film the property, not people.** Keep customers, kids and neighbors out of
  the picture.
- **Your phone's space is safe.** Each video is small. It is deleted from your
  phone once it is sent. It never goes into your photos.

## Trouble

Find what the screen says, then do what is next to it.

| The screen says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| TestFlight asks for a code, or the second email never came | Tell the office you accepted the first email. Look in Junk. Ask the office to send it again. |
| That code is not right. 0 tries left. | Tap **SEND A NEW CODE**. Only the newest code works. |
| We could not send a code right now. | Wait a minute and try again. Still stuck: call the office. |
| Is this the right stop? | Right house and right customer: tap **SAVE ANYWAY**. Wrong stop: tap **PICK THE RIGHT STOP**. |
| You look far from this address | At the right house: tap **YES, STILL RIGHT**. |
| Camera is turned off | Tap **OPEN SETTINGS**. Switch on Camera and Microphone. Go back and tap the stop again. |
| WAITING TO SEND | Nothing. It sends when you have signal. Open the app and leave it open a minute. |
| SENT TO OFFICE | Nothing. Your video is safe and the office has it. |
| 1 video not saved yet | Tap **FINISH IT**, then **SAVE**. |
| A call or alarm stopped this video | Tap **SAVE** if it is good enough, or **RECORD AGAIN**. |
| Your phone is almost full | Tap **RECORD ANYWAY**. Clear some space that evening. |
| Your phone is full | Delete some photos or apps, never Zest Video. Then tap **TRY AGAIN**. |
| Please update Zest Video | Open the **TestFlight** app, tap Zest Video, tap **Update**. |
| NOT SENT | Call the office. Do not delete the app. |
| The Sign In screen shows by itself | Call the office. |
| Anything else | Call the office. |
